Questions

It means we provide routable public IP addresses as a service. The addresses are announced from our network and delivered to you over a WireGuard tunnel, so you do not need to own address space or run BGP yourself.

Anycast announces the same IP address from more than one location. Clients are sent to a nearby one, and if a location goes away, traffic goes to another. It suits stateless or replicated services such as DNS, APIs and edge workers.

WireGuard is encrypted, simple to configure and runs well on small servers, routers and cloud instances. A tunnel works from almost anywhere you can run Linux, VyOS or a WireGuard client.

Homelab and Startup plans run up to 1 Gbps per tunnel, and Business includes up to 2.5 Gbps, with 5 and 10 Gbps available by quote using parallel tunnels. Speed is burst capacity, not a promise for every moment: it also depends on the hardware and the internet connection at your end, and a single flow is limited by one CPU core at each end.
