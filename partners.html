How commission is calculated

The arithmetic, at list price

These figures are arithmetic, not results. Netquor has no partner payout history yet, no partner has earned these amounts, and most will earn less. Discounts, free trials, annual billing and unpaid invoices lower the real number.

ExampleCalculationResult
One referred customer, 8 Macs and 8 iPhones8 x $5.95 + 8 x $3.95 = $79.20 a month. 20% = $15.84$15.84 a month, $190.08 over 12 months
Ten referred customers like that10 x $79.20 = $792.00 a month. 20% = $158.40$158.40 a month, $1,900.80 over 12 months
If those ten customers pay annuallyAnnual Fleet pays for 10 months of the monthly price: $7,920.00 a year. 20% = $1,584.00$1,584.00 for the year
If a customer qualifies for startup pricingHalf price for 12 months, so the base halves: $39.60 a month. 20% = $7.92$7.92 a month for that customer

Commission is calculated on net cash collected, after any discount the customer receives, so partner terms do not add a discount of their own. Net excludes tax, refunds, chargebacks, credits and processing fees.

The rules

What partners agree to

Register each lead

Email us the business and contact. We confirm in writing that it is new to Netquor. Attribution lasts 90 days from that confirmation.

New customers only

Existing customers, accounts in a trial, and businesses you own or are affiliated with do not qualify.

Cash only

Commission is paid on cash collected, never on invoices paid with credit.

Paid after the dispute window

Monthly in arrears, 60 days after the customer's payment, once refund and dispute windows have closed. Balances under $25 roll forward. We need a W-9 before the first payout.

Say that you get paid

If you recommend Netquor and earn commission, disclose it clearly in the same message. The FTC requires this for any material connection.

No exclusivity

You can work with other vendors, and customers can buy from Netquor directly. Programs and rates may change with notice, and earned commission is honored.

Questions

Common questions

Is there a fee to join?

No. There is no fee to apply or to join as a Referral partner. Resellers and Managed partners go through a short onboarding with us.

How much could I earn?

It depends on how many devices and services your referred customers use, and for how long. The arithmetic above shows how commission is calculated at list price. It is not a promise, and we have no partner earnings history yet.

Do I support the customers?

Referral partners do not. Resellers and Managed partners handle first-line support for their own customers. Netquor supports partners by email and does not publish a service-level agreement.

Does this cover Netquor Phone?

Netquor Phone is in early access and not billed yet. Partner terms for it will be set after billing starts.

How do I apply?

Email hello@netquor.com with your business, the kind of customers you work with, and which tier you are interested in. We reply in writing.
