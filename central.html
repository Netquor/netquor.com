Netquor Central · in development

People, groups, inbox filters and sign-in, in one place

Netquor Central is the dashboard Netquor runs its own team on. It keeps your directory and Google Workspace in step, works with your single sign-on service, manages groups, Gmail filters, labels and signatures, and reacts when someone joins, moves or leaves.

Tell us about your Workspace See what is built

Not sold as a product yet. We use it every day, and we are shaping it with a few Google Workspace teams.

What Netquor Central does

What is built, and what is not

Most of this runs Netquor today. Netquor Central is not yet a product you can buy: it has no published price or self-serve sign-up. This is the state of each part.

How it fits

One source of truth for people

Your directory owns the facts about each person. Netquor Central carries them to Google Workspace, and your sign-in service keeps everyone on one login.

Safe by design

It shows you the change before it makes it

Preview first

Policies can be previewed, and so can the Google user, ID and group syncs. New policies start switched off.

Leavers are suspended

Accounts are suspended, never deleted, so nothing is lost to a Google license grace period.

Limits on every run

A cap on new accounts and suspensions per run limits the damage a bug can do.

Sandboxed scripts

Policy scripts run in a sandbox with access only to the functions Netquor Central lists.

Mail stays inside

Filter forwarding is limited to your own domain unless you allow an address.

Every change is recorded

Each sync keeps a run record and writes audit entries. Passwords are never logged.

Questions

Common questions

Who is Netquor Central for?

Small and mid-size organizations on Google Workspace that want one place to run people, groups, Gmail filters, signatures and sign-in. It suits an owner or IT admin who is tired of updating the same person in three places and of hand-building Gmail filters one mailbox at a time.

Is it available today?

Not as a product. Netquor runs Netquor Central for its own team, and we are shaping it with a few Google Workspace teams. There is no published price yet, so the way in today is to tell us about your Workspace.

Do I need LDAP or Authentik?

Netquor Central is designed around a directory as the source of truth and works with LDAP, Authentik and Keycloak. If you do not have one, tell us what you use, and we will say honestly whether Netquor Central fits.

Does it use AI?

One feature does. An administrator picks example emails and Netquor Central proposes up to three Gmail filter rules for them. Nothing is created until the administrator reviews and saves a rule, and the text of the emails is treated as data, never as instructions.

What does it need from Google?

A service account with domain-wide delegation. The scopes depend on the features you turn on: directory and group access for sync, Gmail settings for filters and signatures, and broader mail access only for backfill and retention. Recovery details are never touched.
