Use more than one product, pay less

Use two Netquor products together and take 10% off their plan fees. Use three and take 15%. It covers Netquor Fleet, Netquor VPN and, when it starts billing, Netquor Phone. Netquor SDN bundles are quoted per site.

Our commitment

Small business first. Homelab always.

We serve small businesses across Fargo-Moorhead and the Midwest, and we are proud champions of the homelab community: the people who learn by building. We vouch for learning networking, security and programming, and we build our products so that what you learn on them works anywhere.

Routing, VLANs, BGP and WireGuard on standard, open tools like VyOS and Linux, so the skills carry to any network you touch.

Programming

Common questions

Where do you work?

Netquor is based in Fargo, North Dakota. On-site installation and repair covers Fargo-Moorhead and the Midwest. Hosted services such as Netquor Fleet and Network Solutions are delivered over the internet.

Do I need a contract?

Network Solutions and Netquor VPN are month to month, and Hosted Home Assistant can be cancelled any time. Netquor Fleet starts with a free 30-day trial and has no minimum number of devices.

Is Netquor Phone available today?

It is in early access. You can join as a founding partner at phone.netquor.com. Billing is not built yet, so nobody is charged during early access, and the first 100 accounts keep the launch price for 24 months. The sample calls on that site are scripted, and the product has no customer case studies yet.

Do you offer student pricing?

Yes. The Network Solutions Homelab plan is half price for students and learners.

How do I get a quote for managed networking?
