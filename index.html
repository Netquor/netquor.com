Our commitment

Small business first. Homelab always.

We serve small businesses across Fargo-Moorhead and the Midwest, and we are proud champions of the homelab community: the people who learn by building. We vouch for learning networking, security and programming, and we build our products so that what you learn on them works anywhere.

Networking

Routing, VLANs, BGP and WireGuard on standard, open tools like VyOS and Linux, so the skills carry to any network you touch.

Security

Firewalls, VPNs, patching and backups, explained plainly. Ask us why we set something up the way we did. Questions are welcome.

Programming

Our SDN and Fleet platforms have APIs you can script against, and our configs are plain text you can read, change and learn from.
