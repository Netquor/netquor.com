Apple device readiness

For teams with Macs, iPhones and iPads

Seven practices that keep a small fleet manageable. Answer for how things work today.

  1. You can list every device and who has it.
  2. New devices are set up from a standard configuration, not by hand.
  3. Staff can install approved apps without asking you.
  4. Operating system and app updates happen on a schedule you control.
  5. You can block software that should not run.
  6. You can run a script or install a package on a device without visiting it.
  7. VPN and Wi-Fi settings reach devices without touching each one.

Call handling readiness

For owners who miss calls while working

Seven practices for making sure a caller reaches someone or gets an answer.

  1. Every business call is answered or returned the same day.
  2. After-hours callers get an answer or can leave a message.
  3. You know what each caller wanted without listening to every voicemail.
  4. Callers reach the right person or department.
  5. A second person can pick up when you cannot.
  6. You keep a record of missed calls and callbacks.
  7. Your number stays yours if you change provider.

Network and remote access readiness

For offices, labs and anyone hosting from home

Seven practices for a network you can see, secure and reach from anywhere.

  1. You can list every device on your network.
  2. Guest, staff and device traffic are on separate networks.
  3. Remote access uses a VPN, not open ports.
  4. You have a public IP address you control for hosting.
  5. Your firewall rules are written down and reviewed.
  6. Someone notices when something goes down.
  7. Your router and switch configuration is backed up.

People and access readiness

For teams on Google Workspace, and for buildings

Seven practices for starting and stopping access without hunting through tools.

  1. Adding a person updates every system from one place.
  2. A leaver loses access the same day.
  3. Email signatures are consistent and updated automatically.
  4. Group membership is managed in one place, not in each tool.
  5. Inbox filters and labels are shared across the organization.
  6. Door access can be changed without replacing hardware.
  7. Visitors and guests can be given temporary access.
