# Netquor Technologies

> Fargo, North Dakota technology company serving the Midwest. Managed networking (Ubiquiti UniFi, VyOS, WireGuard), Apple device management, routable IP space on our own network (AS403008), VPN, hosted Home Assistant, building access control, and a virtual receptionist and business phone system in early access.

Netquor Technologies LLC is based in Fargo, North Dakota and serves small businesses across Fargo-Moorhead, North Dakota, Minnesota and the Midwest. ## Bundles
Use two Netquor products together and take 10% off their monthly plan fees; use three and take 15%. Eligible: Netquor Fleet, Netquor VPN and, when it starts billing, Netquor Phone. Netquor SDN bundles are quoted per site. Usage is never discounted, including Phone AI minutes. Annual and volume pricing does not combine with the bundle discount; the lower price applies. Netquor Phone is in early access and not yet billed. Netquor VPN is WireGuard and Fleet can push a WireGuard tunnel configuration to chosen devices. Manage Netquor subscriptions at https://account.netquor.com/.
