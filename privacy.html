Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Netquor Technologies collects, uses, stores, discloses, and protects information when you visit netquor.com or use our related products and services. It is structured around ten core privacy and disclosure clauses, including contact and request-handling provisions.

1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to netquor.com and any Netquor-owned websites, hosted tools, applications, forms, communications, or service interactions that reference this Policy (collectively, the “Services”). It does not apply to third-party websites, platforms, or services that may be linked from our Services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, company name, email address, phone number, billing details, support messages, and any information you choose to submit through forms, email, or service inquiries.

We may also collect technical and operational data such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, timestamps, and usage activity needed to operate, secure, and improve the Services.

3. How We Use Information

Netquor may use collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and support the Services
  • Respond to inquiries, quotes, and customer support requests
  • Process transactions and service relationships
  • Monitor performance, security, and abuse prevention
  • Improve website functionality, user experience, and operations
  • Comply with legal, contractual, and regulatory obligations

4. Disclosure and Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Your mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes. We may disclose information to service providers, infrastructure vendors, payment processors, professional advisers, or government authorities when reasonably necessary to operate the Services, fulfill obligations, enforce agreements, protect rights, investigate misuse, or comply with applicable law.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our Services may use cookies, analytics tools, logs, and similar technologies to remember preferences, measure traffic, improve performance, and maintain security. You may control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may affect site functionality.

We use Google Analytics on netquor.com to understand which pages are visited and how visitors find us. It sets cookies and collects usage information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type. We have turned off Google's advertising and cross-device features. Analytics does not run if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, and you can also block it with Google's opt-out browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout or with your browser's cookie settings.

6. Data Retention and Data Lifecycle Controls

We retain personal and operational information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including service delivery, recordkeeping, security, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. We apply practical data lifecycle controls, including access limitation, retention review, and deletion or anonymization where appropriate.

7. Security Safeguards

Netquor uses commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of certain personal information. You may also request information about how your data has been used or shared. We will review and respond to verified requests in accordance with applicable law.

9. Third-Party Services and Cross-Border Processing

Some data may be processed by third-party providers that help us host infrastructure, process payments, monitor site performance, or deliver communications. Those providers may operate in jurisdictions different from your own. When using such providers, we take reasonable steps to ensure information is handled in a manner consistent with our business obligations and this Policy.

10. Contact Information and Privacy Requests

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to submit a privacy request, or need to update your contact information, you may contact Netquor Technologies using the contact details made available through netquor.com.

Business Contact: Netquor Technologies, Fargo, North Dakota.

Privacy requests should include enough detail for us to identify your relationship with Netquor and evaluate your request. We may ask for additional information to verify identity before fulfilling sensitive requests.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
