The rules in plain words

Who qualifies and how it works

Who qualifies

A new Netquor customer that is under two years old and has fewer than ten people, with a work email on its own domain. Any kind of business, funded or not.

Your own devices

The discount covers your own team's devices, up to 25. It does not cover devices you manage for clients.

The better price, not both

Startup pricing replaces the bundle, annual and volume discounts while it runs. If one of those would be cheaper for you, you get that one instead.

No surprises at the end

The discount ends on its own after 12 months. We email you 30 days before, and the confirmation states the price you pay afterwards.

Free devices

Fleet's earned free devices do not combine with the startup price. You keep whichever is lower.

Terms can change

We may change or end the program with notice. Enrollments already approved are honored for their term, and we may decline or end one for a false application.

How to apply

Three steps

  1. Email us. Company, website or work email domain, year founded and headcount, device counts and any other products you are curious about.
  2. We reply in writing. We confirm eligibility and the exact price. There is no application portal, so we answer by email.
  3. Start the free trial. Fleet is free for 30 days. The startup price begins with your first paid month.

Questions

Common questions

Do we need funding or an accelerator?

No. Funding stage and type of business do not matter. Agencies and consultancies can join for their own devices.

Can an existing customer join?

Not today. The program is for new customers. If you are already a customer and think you should qualify, write to us.

What happens to Netquor Phone?

Netquor Phone is in early access and not billed yet. When billing starts, Phone has its own startup program: 50% off the plan fee for 6 months, with usage never discounted. Its price here is a planned launch price.

Can I combine this with a referral credit?

A referral credit applies to plan fees after other discounts, and it cannot take a line below half of its list price. While startup pricing is already at half, a referral credit waits until it ends. See the referral terms.

How long does a reply take?

We reply by email, usually within a few business days. We do not promise a faster time.

Starting something?

Tell us what you are building and what you need to run it. We will reply with a plan and a price in writing.
