Your smart home, managed and reachable anywhere

Keep your integrations cloud-native. We run your assistant in the cloud for access from anywhere, and we rent you a small bridge device that connects it to your home network. Updates, backups and support from a local team.

How it works

1

Pick a plan

Choose how much assistant you need. You can move up or down any month.

2

We ship the bridge

A small, pre-configured device arrives at your door. It is rented, so there is nothing to buy.

3

Plug it in

Power and a network cable to your router. It connects your home to your assistant over a secure, encrypted link.

4

Open it anywhere

Your assistant is online. We can restore a backup from your existing setup so nothing is lost.
